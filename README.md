
# Quantum Simulations

A computational exploration of quantum mechanics 
and quantum information science using Python.

## Motivation

This project aims to deepen my understanding of 
quantum systems through mathematical modelling, 
numerical simulations, and scientific programming.

## Research Interests

- Quantum information and computation
- Quantum entanglement
- Quantum dynamics
- Computational quantum physics

## Project Roadmap

- [ ] Qubit states and normalization
- [ ] Quantum gates and operators
- [ ] Quantum measurements
- [ ] Tensor products and multi-qubit systems
- [ ] Entanglement and Bell states
- [ ] Quantum circuits
- [ ] Quantum algorithms
- [ ] Quantum dynamics

## Tools

- Python
- NumPy
- SciPy
- Matplotlib
- pytest

## About

Developed as an independent computational physics 
project alongside my MSc studies in Physics at 
Lund University.
